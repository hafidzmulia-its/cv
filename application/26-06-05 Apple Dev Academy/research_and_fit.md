## Official Program Signals

- Applications for Cohort 2027 are open until 15 June 2026, with Indonesian Academy locations including Bali, Batam, Central Jakarta, Surabaya, and Tangerang.
- The Academy is open for learners aged 18+ from broad backgrounds.
- The Academy focuses on coding, design, and professional skills.
- The program is described as a 9-month journey that helps participants develop their own apps and become well-rounded professionals.
- The program emphasizes leadership, collaboration, communication, and becoming a T-shaped professional.
- Apple's Developer Academies page says students complete the app development cycle multiple times, practice collaboration and presentation, identify community problems, and solve them with apps that enrich people's lives.
- The official knowledge base describes the program as using Challenge-Based Learning, where learners identify meaningful real-world challenges, ask questions, investigate deeply, and create innovative solutions.

## Portfolio Guidance Extract

CV:
- Submit a CV no more than 2 pages in PDF format.
- File name guidance: FullName_CV_Academy.
- CV may include work experience, education, extracurricular experience, publications, skills, and awards.

Portfolio:
- Submit maximum 5 projects.
- Provide in English.
- For each project, include a one- or two-sentence summary.
- State whether the project was self-initiated, class assignment, or work assignment.
- If it was a group project, clearly state it and explain the applicant's role.
- Explain impact and learning.
- Add images or links when useful.
- File name guidance: FullName_Portfolio_Academy.

What they look for:
- Interest and motivation for the 9-month journey.
- Creativity and expression.
- Interdisciplinary potential.
- Work ethic and excellence.
- Growth and learning process, not only polished final projects.
